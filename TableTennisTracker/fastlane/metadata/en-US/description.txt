Track Your Table Tennis Training Like a Pro

Table Tennis Training Tracker is the training companion for players of all levels. Build consistent habits, record every match you play, and watch your game improve over time.

LOG SESSIONS IN SECONDS

Record your training with all the details that matter:

- Duration - anything from 5 to 300 minutes
- Session type - Technique, Match Play, Tournament, Serve Practice, Physical, Free Play or Other
- RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion) - rate intensity from 1 to 10 to monitor training load
- Notes - drills practiced, observations, areas to improve
- Date - log today's session or backfill past training days

RECORD MATCHES AND OPPONENTS

Every session can hold the matches you played:

- Game-by-game scores, singles or doubles
- Competition level - Practice, League or Tournament
- Flag the matches that were ranked
- Keep an opponent list with club, rating, handedness and playing style - Attacker, Defender, All-round, Pips or Chopper
- Per-match notes, so you remember what actually worked

ANALYTICS THAT ANSWER REAL QUESTIONS

- Win rate and win/loss record across every match you log
- Match results chart
- Weekly training time, with totals and averages
- A 12-month training heatmap that shows your consistency at a glance
- Choose which widgets appear on your analytics screen

SMART CALENDAR VIEW

- Switch between month and week views
- Visual indicators show session density at a glance
- Tap any date to see that day's sessions
- Set your own first day of the week
