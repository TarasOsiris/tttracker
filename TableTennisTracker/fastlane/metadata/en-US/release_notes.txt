Numbers now follow the language you choose in the app. Training durations, percentages and scores used to stay in your device's language even after you switched — they don't any more.

VoiceOver can read the calendar properly: every day now announces its full date and how many sessions it holds, instead of just a number. The training heatmap reads the same way, and it scrolls more smoothly than before.

Opponent ratings accept decimals again — a rating typed with a comma is no longer dropped when you save.

Buttons that were too small to hit comfortably are now full size, and text no longer clips at larger accessibility text sizes.
